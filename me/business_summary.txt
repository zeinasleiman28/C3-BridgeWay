Bridgeway – Fundraising & Communications Consultancy for NGOs is a small consultancy in Hamra, Beirut, Lebanon. Our mission is to help Lebanese NGOs reach donors, partners and volunteers with honest, respectful outreach. What sets us apart: we know the region's donor landscape (EU grants, Gulf foundations, the Lebanese diaspora), we write ethical, non-pushy messages, we never buy email lists, we work in Arabic, English and French, and we report results transparently.

Our services are priced in USD per campaign. A Donor outreach campaign is 600 (research on up to 100 donor prospects plus a 3-email sequence). A Partnership pitch pack is 450 (up to 30 target organizations, with a pitch email and a one-page summary). A Volunteer drive is 300 (4 weeks of social posts, emails and a sign-up form). Grant-deadline tracking is 80 per month (a monthly alert of relevant grants and their deadlines). Add-ons: Arabic/French translation 50, and an extra follow-up wave 100.

Discounts: small NGOs with an annual budget under 100,000 USD get 20% off, and booking 2 or more services gets 10% off. Discounts do not stack: when both apply, we apply the bigger one. For example, a donor outreach campaign for a small NGO is 600 minus 20% = 480.

How it works: we start with a free 30-minute discovery call, launch the campaign within 2 weeks, and send a results report at the end. We only contact public or opted-in addresses, and we never promise how much money a campaign will raise. We do not write full grant proposals, buy or sell email lists, run paid ads, handle or collect donation money, or do outreach for political parties or for-profit companies.
